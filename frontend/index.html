The 5 Pillars of Trading Excellence

"Trading is 20% strategy, 80% psychology"

1 Master One Strategy First

Stop jumping between systems. Find one strategy that aligns with your personality and market view. Master it completely before exploring others. Consistency comes from specialization.

2 Journal Every Single Trade

Document trades, emotions, market conditions, and mistakes. Your trading journal is your most valuable teacher. Review it weekly to identify patterns in your behavior and performance.

3 Risk Management is Supreme

Never risk more than 1-2% of your capital per trade. Protect your account like your life depends on it. You can't recover from a blown account, but you can always make back small losses.

4 Quality Over Quantity

One high-probability setup is worth ten mediocre trades. Be patient. Wait for your edge. The market rewards those who wait for the perfect pitch, not those who swing at everything.

5 Never Stop Evolving

Markets evolve constantly. What worked yesterday may not work tomorrow. Commit to continuous learning, adaptation, and improvement. Stagnation is the enemy of success.
