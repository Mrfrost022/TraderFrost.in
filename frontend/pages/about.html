My Journey

My name is Prashant Rajput — a Forex trader, trading psychology coach, and mentor. With over three years of full-time experience in the Forex market and a strong foundation in Smart Money Concepts (SMC), I have trained and guided more than 100 students toward becoming informed, disciplined, and independent traders.

This book is not just a story of my personal journey — it's a comprehensive resource. Inside, you will find complete and structured knowledge of the Forex market: from basic concepts to institutional-level strategies, deep insights into Smart Money trading, and the often-overlooked but critical aspect of trading psychology.
